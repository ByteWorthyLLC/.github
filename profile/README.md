## What we do

Small firms sit on rooms of paper nobody can search: closed policy
files, transaction records, pre-need contracts, tax workpapers. We
come to you, count the boxes, and tell you what storage is costing
you. Then we digitize on-site, reconcile every box against a signed
manifest with a checksum, and install a searchable archive on
equipment you own. Every answer it gives is cited back to the page
image it came from.

We keep no copy of your records. That is the point.

## The free 30-minute audit

A box count, a category breakdown, what storage costs you per year,
and which categories are past their retention window. The findings
are yours in writing whether or not you hire us.
